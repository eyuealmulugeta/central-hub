Privacy policy

Amistad Central Hub, the internal business application of Amistad Prefab. Last updated 19 October 2026.

Who this is for

Amistad Central Hub is a private tool used by the staff, site engineers and partners of Amistad Prefab to run construction projects, trading, finance, documents and people records. It is not a public service. Only people the company has approved can sign in.

What we collect

  • Your Google account name and email address, when you sign in with Google. We use them only to know who you are and what you may open.
  • Records that people enter into the application: project data, daily reports, schedules, costs, invoices, contracts, staff records, contacts (names, email addresses and phone numbers), messages and similar business information.
  • Technical records such as which device is signed in, and problems the application meets, so that they can be fixed.

We do not collect payment card details, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Where it is kept

Records are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), a service of Google LLC, in the project of the company. Some settings and a copy of recent records are also kept in your own browser on your device so that the application works with a weak connection. Access to every record is limited by the rules of the company: a person sees only what their role and the projects assigned to them allow.

Who sees it

Your data is seen only by the people of Amistad Prefab who need it for their work, and by Google as the provider of the sign-in and database service. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with other companies, except when a document you ask the application to send (an invoice, a report, an email) is sent to the person you chose.

How long we keep it

Business records are kept for as long as the company needs them for its work and for the records the law requires. Staff and contact details are removed when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to be removed from the application, by writing to info@amistadprefab.com. You can also remove the application's access to your Google account at any time in your Google account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date.
